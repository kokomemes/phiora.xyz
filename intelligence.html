02 / Intelligence

Explore. Reason. Insight.

Three proposed pillars of a general intelligence experience. These are directions for exploration, not launched or production-ready modules.

01 / EXPLORE

Begin with the question.

Explore is envisioned as a research experience for collecting context, summarizing material, highlighting evidence, and identifying gaps worth investigating.

Research · Sources · Context
02 / REASON

Examine the alternatives.

Reason is a proposed framework for evaluating options against explicit criteria, reviewing assumptions, and understanding the trade-offs behind different paths.

Comparisons · Assumptions · Trade-offs
03 / INSIGHT

See what matters.

Insight is envisioned as a way to present findings through concise reports, visual structures, and accessible explanations that users can inspect and challenge.

Summaries · Visuals · Clarity
