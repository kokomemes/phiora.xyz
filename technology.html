03 / Architecture

Built around questions.

Our technical direction prioritizes transparent reasoning, useful interfaces, and verifiable outputs over exaggerated autonomy claims.

I

Grounded inputs

Future research workflows should distinguish provided material, sourced evidence, and generated interpretation.

II

Visible logic

Analysis should make assumptions, criteria, and uncertainty easier to inspect.

III

Human judgment

Outputs should support decisions rather than claim authority over them.

Design principles

No black-box promises.

PHIORA is currently a product concept. There is no claim of a proprietary model, deployed infrastructure, autonomous execution, or finished AI application.

Future prototypes will need practical testing for reliability, usability, and clear boundaries.
